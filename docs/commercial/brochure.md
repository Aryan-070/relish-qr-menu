## Why restaurants switch

A printed menu is a missed opportunity. It can't show the dish, specials lag behind reprints, guests wait for a waiter to ask a question, and you end the night with no data on what sold, when, or why. Relish fixes all four — on the phone already in your guest's hand.

### Five pillars
1. **Cinematic guest experience** — seven designer themes, a hero film, and per-dish video. Guests feel the brand before the first bite.
2. **AI that upsells for you** — a mood / budget / party-size recommender raises average check with zero staff effort.
3. **Tableside service, digitized** — call waiter, request water, split the bill, add gratuity, see Jain & allergy info — fewer wait-times, happier tables.
4. **A back-office that pays for itself** — live floor map, full menu control, real analytics (peak hours, top items, category mix, staff performance).
5. **Premium, but priced for India** — one all-in fee from ₹15,000, then a yearly renewal. No POS rip-out, no app to download. Your guests just scan.

**Guest QR App** — 7 landing/hero themes, full menu booklet, item detail sheets (photo, pairings, customizations), AI recommendation flow, cart & order review, service panel (waiter/water/split bill/gratuity), Jain/veg/allergy & spice labelling, per-dish & per-screen cinematic video, 3 switchable visual themes.
