Video egress on Cloudflare R2 — the hosting cost lever

Relish charges a single all-inclusive one-time fee to design, build and launch the menu — including the costly, multi-pass AI video generation. The annual renewal (₹2,999–4,999/yr) keeps the menu hosted, supported and updated — and because cost-to-serve is tiny, it carries a high margin and builds a real recurring revenue base on top of the build. Charging one number plus a yearly renewal, not a stack of line items, is what makes it pitchable.

The wedge: no competitor in India bundles cinematic / AI-generated video into a clean one-time price. Relish absorbs the expensive part (video that rarely lands in one pass) and gives only a small discount when the restaurant supplies its own media — so a bring-your-own-video build is actually our highest-margin job.

Competitors sell monthly SaaS (Petpooja ₹625–833/mo; global players ₹3,300–10,000/mo). Relish sells a one-time build + nominal annual renewal — a different, easier-to-say-yes-to shape, premium on the build (justified by cinematic + video + AI) and almost free to keep. And Relish is alone in bundling cinematic video into one price.
